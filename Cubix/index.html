Cubix: An Overview

Cubix is a one in all website for cubers. It tells you about various cubes,the methods to solve and also provides videos for the same.

Rubix cube was invented in 1974 by Ernő Rubik, a Hungarian architect and professor of architecture. He created the cube as a teaching tool to help his students understand three-dimensional geometry. A puzzle with over 43 quintillion possible configurations, the Rubik's Cube has become one of the best-selling puzzles in the world.

Cubix exists to serve the purpose of providing a platform for cubers to learn about cubes and methods to solve them.
